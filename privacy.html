Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Glycolysis Interactive is an educational website designed for learning about the glycolysis metabolic pathway. This privacy policy explains how we handle any data collected through your use of this site.

Data Collection

This website is a static educational resource. We do not collect personal information, do not use cookies for tracking, and do not operate user accounts.

The site does not transmit data to third-party analytics services. No browsing data is sold, shared, or used for advertising purposes.

Local Storage

This website does not currently use browser local storage, cookies, or similar technologies. If a future version stores viewer preferences (such as hydrogen visibility or rotation settings) in local storage, that data would remain entirely on your device and would never be transmitted to any server, and this policy would be updated to describe it.

Third-Party Services

The 3D molecular structures displayed on this site are sourced from PubChem, a public chemical database maintained by the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI). Loading these structures may involve fetching SDF files from the site's own server; no user data is sent to external services during this process.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revision date at the top of this page will be changed. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
